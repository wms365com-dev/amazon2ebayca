Sources:

- https://www.replencatcher.com/

Important note:

- It does not independently verify that each feature works as described.

## Core Product Positioning

Replen Catcher presents itself as an `eBay to Amazon` sourcing and replenishment platform focused on automating deal discovery from eBay for Amazon sellers.

The product promise is not just "search eBay." It is framed as:

- a live sourcing stream
- a replens-list monitoring system
- an automation layer that reduces manual spreadsheet work
- a tool for finding both current and future opportunities

## Discovery Features Promised

- Live stream sourcing from a large eBay deal pool
- Access to over 2 million monitored eBay products
- Ability to source deals even without an existing replens list
- Category, ROI, and profit filtering on the live stream
- Ability to uncover "new niches" with less competition
- Ability to track profitable products that other tools allegedly do not include

## Replens Monitoring Features Promised

- Bulk upload of top-selling Amazon products by ASIN
- Monitoring of a replens list on eBay `24/7`
- Matching uploaded Amazon items to eBay listings
- Notifications when deals meet minimum ROI and profit criteria
- Monitoring of hundreds or thousands of ASINs
- Viewing all available eBay offers for monitored replens in one place
- Ability to track any desired product, rather than only a limited catalog subset

## Pricing, Alerting, and Decision Features Promised

- Set target buy prices on replens
- Receive alerts when products reach those target prices
- Identify seasonal or temporarily unprofitable products that may become profitable later
- Access to multiple Amazon price data points for decisions, including:
  - lowest offer
  - lowest FBA
  - current buy box
  - 30-day buy box
  - 90-day buy box

## Speed and Workflow Features Promised

- Reduction in sourcing time by `50-70%`
- Faster sourcing for the top `10%` of items via `Top Seller Speed Mode`
- Higher sourcing productivity for virtual assistants
- Elimination of spreadsheet-based monitoring
- More efficient replenishment sourcing through automation

## Matching and Data Quality Features Promised

- Per-ASIN mismatch fixing for eBay/Amazon matches
- Faster sourcing by reducing time spent on mismatches
- More accurate data for sourcing decisions

## Marketplace and Sourcing Mode Features Promised

- Sourcing from eBay auctions
- Auction filtering
- Ability to place bids near listing close
- Support for `buy it now`, `best offer`, and auction-style sourcing workflows

## Amazon Account / Integration Features Promised

- Direct Amazon seller account integration
- Ability to filter out restricted product results
- Ability to add Amazon orders to the replens list

## Anti-Counterfeit / Specialty Features Promised

- Books-specific counterfeit checking
- One-click check against the Booksrun counterfeit calculator

## Business Outcome Claims Promised

- "More replens = more sales"
- Ability to source thousands of products from eBay to Amazon
- Claim that one seller sourced over 6,000 items and sold over $700,000 on Amazon in the prior 12 months
- Promise that the feature set helps users find opportunities competitors are missing

## Product Shape Summary

If we use Replen Catcher as a benchmark, the product it promises is built around these pillars:

1. Live opportunity stream
2. Bulk ASIN import and monitored replens list
3. Persistent monitoring and alerting
4. Faster sourcing for priority items
5. Mismatch correction and richer pricing signals
6. Auction sourcing support
7. Amazon-account-connected workflow

## Implications For Our Build

To move closer to this benchmark, our product should prioritize:

1. Monitored replens list and bulk ASIN import
2. Target buy prices and alerts
3. Exact-ASIN monitoring workflows
4. Mismatch review tools
5. Priority item / top seller acceleration
6. Auction-aware sourcing
7. Amazon account sync for restrictions and order-based replen tracking
